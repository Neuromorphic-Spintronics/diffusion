A stochastic dynamical system defines a probability distribution over trajectories.
We present \texttt{diffusionsde}, a Python package that fits conditional trajectory
distributions from simulated or measured ensembles. A one-dimensional conditional
U-Net predicts the noise added by a variance-preserving diffusion process. The
package supports ancestral DDPM sampling and reduced-step DDIM sampling. With
$\eta=0$ and fixed initial noise, the DDIM update is deterministic. Trajectories
and conditions may be scalar or multichannel; conditions can also contain full
driving waveforms. We define the data representation, training objective, reverse
samplers, validation statistics, and differentiable inverse workflow. An
Ornstein--Uhlenbeck process provides analytic and finite-step stationary references.
A stochastically forced Duffing oscillator tests nonlinear transient dynamics with
coloured forcing. The same workflow is then applied to measured magnetic-device
trajectories for which a trusted governing equation is not available.
